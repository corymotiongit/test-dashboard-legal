Terms of Service

test-dashboard is a private, internal proof-of-concept tool used by its owner to view the metrics of his own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

The app only reads public metrics (followers, video views, likes, comments and shares) from the owner's own TikTok account, with the owner's consent. Data is stored locally on the owner's machine, is not shared with third parties, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or AI training. To request deletion, contact the owner.
